Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 26, 2026 | App: Jamaat on Time

Welcome to Jamaat on Time. Your privacy is deeply important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

  • Location Data: With your permission, the app accesses your precise and coarse location (GPS) solely while the app is in use to calculate distances to nearby masājid and display them on the map. We do not track your location in the background or store your location history on remote servers.
  • Account & Feedback Data: If you sign in via Google to contribute timings or submit contact messages, we collect basic profile info (Name, Email) to authenticate voluntary timing edits and respond to feedback.

2. How We Use Information

  • To display nearby masājid and accurate Jama'at timings relative to your current location.
  • To manage crowd-sourced Jama'at timing contributions and prevent spam.
  • To respond to user support messages and feedback.

3. Third-Party Services

Our app utilizes trusted third-party services that may collect information used to identify you:

  • Google Maps SDK & Play Services
  • Firebase Services (Firestore, Auth)

4. Data Retention & Security

We implement security best practices to protect your data. Location data processed locally is cleared when the app is closed. We do not sell or share personal data with third-party advertisers.

5. Children's Privacy

Our application does not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under 13.
