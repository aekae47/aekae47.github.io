Account & Data Deletion Request

App: Jamaat on Time | Developer: Tranquility Software

At Tranquility Software, we respect your right to privacy and control over your personal information. If you wish to delete your Jamaat on Time user account and all associated personal data, you can submit a deletion request below or via email.

1. Steps to Request Account & Data Deletion

You can request account deletion using either of the following two options:

Option A: Online Deletion Request Form (Instant Submission)

Fill out the form below. Our system will immediately process and notify our admin team to permanently remove your user record.

✓ Account deletion request submitted successfully! Your account and associated personal data will be completely deleted within 7 days.
✕ Submission failed. Please try again or email us directly at tranquilitysoftw@gmail.com.

Option B: Email Deletion Request

Send an email to tranquilitysoftw@gmail.com with the subject line "Account Deletion Request - Jamaat on Time" from the email address associated with your account.

2. What Data Is Deleted

Upon processing your deletion request, the following user data is permanently purged from our servers (Firebase & Databases):

  • User Account Profile: Your user ID, registered email address, display name, and volunteer status.
  • Personal Favorites & Custom Lists: Your saved masājid bookmarks, custom list categories (Home, Work, etc.), and custom list ordering.
  • Support Messages: Any past contact form messages linked to your email address.

3. What Data Is Retained & Additional Retention Period

  • Crowdsourced Masjid Timings: Publicly submitted Jama'at timings for masājid remain in the app database to serve the community. These records contain no personal user identifiers or account data.
  • Processing Timeline: Deletion requests are processed and completed within 7 business days. Once deleted, your account cannot be recovered.

4. Contact & Support

If you have any questions regarding your data or account deletion, contact Tranquility Software at tranquilitysoftw@gmail.com.
